## UI Design Standard

For all new or changed interface work, follow Apple's Human Interface Guidelines as a
cross-platform design standard. Prioritize clarity, visual hierarchy, familiar
controls, direct feedback, accessible contrast and focus states, responsive layouts,
comfortable touch targets, and purposeful, restrained motion. Keep workflows focused:
reveal complexity progressively, avoid showing unrelated tasks together, and use
plain, action-oriented labels.

When an explicit product requirement conflicts with this guidance, follow the product
requirement and preserve accessibility and usability as far as possible.
